About SGN MicroSolutions Foundation

  • We are a Company Registered Under Sec-8 of the Companies Act in Nov’20 with it’s registered and corporate office in Delhi.
  • We serve small ticket, short term business loan requirement of small retail business owners.
  • We have fully operational 6 branches in 3 states of the country with a workforce of 50+ employees.
  • We have a base of 5000+ unique customers growing on a daily basis.

Our Mission & Vision

  • MISSION: We exist to Empower Small Retail Entrepreneurs by being a trusted partner offering them not just quickly accessible and tailor-made financial solutions but also the support and expertise necessary to fuel their business growth.
  • VISION: We aspire to be the most preferred financial service provider for Small Retail business owners. In the world of small retail, We seek to create a financial ecosystem which serves as a catalyst for dreams realized, businesses thriving and communities flourishing.

    • Our Values

  • Customer-Centric: We prioritize the needs and aspirations of our clients, placing them at the heart of everything we do. Their success is our success.
  • Integrity: We conduct our business with the highest ethical standards, ensuring transparency, honesty, and fairness in all our interactions.
  • Innovation: We constantly seek new and better ways to serve our clients, embracing technology and creative solutions to enhance their experience.
  • Empowerment: We believe in the power of small businesses to drive economic growth and create opportunities. We empower our clients with the financial resources and knowledge to thrive.
  • Community Impact: We are dedicated to giving back to the communities we serve, fostering a positive social and economic impact.
